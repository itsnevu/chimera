WHY TRAITS COME FIRST

Ask a model for “a cat wearing something” a thousand times and you get a thousand images nobody can filter, price or rank. Chimera draws the traits first, against real weights, then writes the prompt from them.

Your metadata therefore describes what you asked for — not what a captioning model guessed afterwards.

WHAT IT WON'T DO

It won't hold your style perfectly. Some editions come back off-model and need re-rolling — and a re-roll is a second charge.

It won't guarantee the picture matches the label. The model can quietly skip a hat you asked for. Metadata matches the request, not the pixels. Review before you mint.

BEFORE YOU SPEND

Layer compositing is free and instant. Every render here is a paid API call and a thousand of them is a three-figure bill that takes hours, not seconds.

Runs resume where they stopped, so a crash never re-bills you for images already on disk.
